Name: Evangelion Turbo - Sumo robot with maze-solving capabilities

I'm designing: sumo robot, with fully custom hardware, software, gearboxes and assembly. It will have 4 distance sensors, 4 line sensors, IMU, EPROM (i want to fit encoders, but we well see about that, it could be too much). It will have about 200W of combined motor power. In the future i want to add maze-solving (micrmouse) capabilities, based on all of these sensors.

Inspo / reference: My inspiration for this project was accually my previous robot - Evangelion (https://github.com/Tacot3589/evangelion). It was okayish robot. Okayish motors, okayish size, okayish assembly. Nothing outstanding. It was very, like very limited by his drivers. It was just slow, not very balanced and a little bit gibberisch (im pround of my job, i learned so much but its trash :). Evangelion Turbo is direct succesor to Evangelion.
